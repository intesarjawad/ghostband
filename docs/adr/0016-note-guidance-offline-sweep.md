# 0016: Retain note guidance 5 after the offline sweep

Status: accepted. The proposed increase to 7 does not generalise across presets.
Live sync acceptance remains unverified.

## Decision

Keep `Guidance.cfg_notes` at 5. The Folk / metal sweep selects 7, and identity
passes at 7, but the matched four-preset confirmation rejects it as a shared
default: three presets regress and the pooled within-120-ms rate falls from
84.7 to 82.2 percent. Do not promote a one-preset winner that reduces the shared
headline measure.

Apply a preset's guidance both when a session connects and when its preset changes,
so the configured setting actually reaches playback. Explicit guidance in user
presets remains supported.

Studio renders now use the shipped bass pulse and onset mode 1. Their timeline
includes frame-zero generation parameters, both preset prompts and the smoothed
prompt blend. Generation blocks on each model frame: machine load changes elapsed
render time, not the number or position of audio samples. Commands land on the first
40 ms frame at or after their scheduled time, with no lookahead.

The offline take adapter copies `Scheduler.beat_log()`, sets `play_time_s=0` and
`dropped_frames=0`, and marks every take `mode=offline`. The live sync protocol
explicitly rejects that mode. Identity scoring also requires `--diagnostic` for
these recordings and never publishes claims from them.

## Evidence

The experiment uses the pinned bundled MRT2 small artifact, all five charts, and
paired seed rotations 1, 2 and 3. Resetting without changing the seed would repeat
the same audio rather than provide three sampled runs.

On Folk / metal, 5 scores 87.1 percent within 120 ms. Requested values 7, 10 and 14
each score 88.1 percent. The initial rule maximises that rate, then minimises median
error and finally the requested guidance value. It selects 7. The median absolute
error increases from 72 to 76 ms; this is a modest rate gain, not an improvement in
every timing measure or a pass of the sync gate.

All 15 paired WAVs at 7, 10 and 14 are bit-identical. The pinned upstream exporter
(`magenta_rt/mlx/export.py::_discretize_cfg_token`, revision
`694a545e4ba0b88bf1150137b129582166d3e07f`) clips guidance tokens to [-1, 7].
The runtime received the requested raw values; higher requests produced no distinct
output for the installed small artifact. This does not impose a new upper bound
on custom preset files or other model exports.

Identity at 7 was measured on four presets, five charts, three conditions, both
slider endpoints and three repeats: 360 takes. All eight preset/slider cells pass,
with normalised identity 0.755 to 0.937. The 120 paired melody comparisons improve
F1 by 0.146 (95 percent interval 0.134 to 0.157). See the script-generated
[identity report](../benchmarks/cfg-identity.md).

The [sweep and matched comparison](../benchmarks/cfg-sweep.md), generated by the
scripts, shows the failed generalisation: Jazz 81.1 to 74.6 percent, Bossa 86.6 to
84.6 percent, Strings 84.1 to 81.6 percent, and Folk 87.1 to 88.1 percent. Each
preset has the same charts, three repeats, slider sweep and 201 eligible changes.
The four-preset confirmation requires identity to pass and the mean preset rate
to improve; equal coverage makes that mean equivalent to the pooled change rate.
It retains 5. Identity passing alone is insufficient evidence for a sync change.

The sync reports also compute their detector-reference row from the FluidSynth
fixtures: 51 of 56 eligible changes within 120 ms (91.1 percent), with three misses.
That is a detector check, not a universal upper bound for generated timbres.

## Limits and reproduction

The 90 percent floor, strict median below 60 ms, miss denominator, null test and
identity thresholds are unchanged. Single chart passes and virtual clocks do not
satisfy the live, duration-compliant zero-drop protocol. Existing published claims
are untouched. Listening quality and a new live acceptance corpus were not measured.

With the local model and permissive extractor installed:

```sh
GHOSTBAND_PERMISSIVE_ENV="$HOME/Library/Application Support/ghostband/permissive" \
uv run python scripts/sweep_cfg.py "$CFG_TAKES" \
  --engine engine/build/ghostband-engine --model-dir "$CFG_MODEL_DIR" \
  --report "${CFG_TAKES}.md"
```

Completed take sidecars are resume markers. Use a fresh output directory after
changing render or model code. WAVs, timelines, sidecars, hashes and verification
logs are retained in the job's `cfg-sweep/` evidence directory. No stronger shared
guidance setting is supported by this experiment.
