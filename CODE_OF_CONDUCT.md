# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome questions, explain disagreements with evidence and give constructive feedback about the work.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language and disclosure of private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation or exclude contributors who violate these rules. Decisions should reflect the severity and pattern of the conduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not disclose another person's private information in a public issue. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and give affected people an opportunity to respond where appropriate.
