# AAM annotation excerpts

`0001_beatinfo.arff` and `0001_segments.arff` are unmodified copies of two annotation files of
track 0001 from the AAM: Artificial Audio Multitracks Dataset, version 1.1.0 (0001-1000
annotations), by Fabian Ostermann and Igor Vatolkin, https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5794629.

The dataset is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). These copies are used only to test the annotation
parser; the audio and the other annotations are not included.
